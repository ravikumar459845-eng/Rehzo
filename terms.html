2. Platform Role

Rehzo provides a digital discovery and request-coordination platform connecting property owners (hosts) and tenants directly. Rehzo does not own or lease properties directly and charges zero brokerage fees.

4. Tenant Responsibilities & Booking TRL

  • The generated 12-digit TRL slip acts as a digital confirmation record between tenant and landlord upon mutual acceptance.
