Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Rehzo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when searching for, listing, or booking verified rental spaces worldwide.

2. Information We Collect

  • Profile Details: Full Name, Contact Number, and optional email address.
  • Google Authentication Data: When you verify with Google, we only receive and store your verified email address and basic profile name provided by Google OAuth. We never collect or store your Google account password.
  • Property & Booking Details: Location coordinates, property addresses, rent figures, and duration selected during room listing or check-in request.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To facilitate direct, broker-free communication between property owners and tenants.
  • To generate official 12-digit TRL (Tenant-Rental-Ledger) confirmation receipts.
  • To protect accounts from abuse and spam.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Contact details are strictly exchanged between verified tenants and property owners upon mutual booking approval. All sensitive credentials are encrypted and securely stored in cloud databases.
